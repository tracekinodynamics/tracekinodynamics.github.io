The problem

Robots have no Common Crawl.

Language and vision models trained on a free, petabyte-scale firehose of human data. Robots have nothing like it. What exists — mocap studios, lab sets, teleoperation, simulation — is expensive, narrow, and missing how people share space, tools, and tasks in the chaos of real work. TRACE builds that missing raw layer.

The pipeline

Capture, verify, license.

01

Multimodal capture

An MMT core — camera, depth, audio, motion — plus a swarm of 6–20 body sensors for full-body pose, every source synchronized to the millisecond.

02

Verified at ingest

Consent-aware, context-aware capture. Every hour clears automated fraud, quality, and policy gates before it enters the WELL.

03

Licensed for training

Cooperative-task data packages for policy training: manipulation, navigation, vehicle and workspace sharing. Provenance and audit built in.

Built and field-proven

The capture stack works today.

  • Records, synchronizes, harvests, and uploads real captured data now
