Become a contributor

TRACE is currently in private alpha. We are selecting early contributors for the first phase of data collection.

Contributors collect real-world task data during normal daily activities using lightweight wearable sensors. No special skills or extra work required. The data is verified, processed, and added to the WELL training dataset for commercial licensing to the robotics industry.

Each hour of approved data earns credits, which represent an earned, transfer-restricted claim to a proportional share of TRACE's licensing revenue — earned only through verified data production, never sold or pre-issued, and valued only within the verified contributor pool. Early adopters earn at up to 64x the standard rate.

What to expect:
- Equipment cost around US$100-200 (open-source design, DIY-friendly)
- Sensor nodes weigh under 20g and are concealable under clothing
- Daily routine: wear sensors, charge at night, data uploads automatically
- No subscription fees or ongoing costs

For details on projected earnings and the market behind them, read the market projections. For answers to common questions, see the extended FAQ.
