TRACE DYNAMICS: A key step towards a prosperous third industrial revolution. 

The future of labor is general purpose robotics. For all of recent history, automation has gradually replaced workers with machinery and advanced processes. The recent advancements in artificial intelligence have accelerated this trend among knowledge workers, and synergy with robotic intelligence is moving rapidly to displace millions of workers, replacing them with general purpose humanoid robots that can be easily re-tasked and shifted as production needs evolve. Humanoid robots are becoming more capable every day, and most work - even in developing nations - can potentially be performed much more economically by robots than by people. 

TRACE offers everyone the opportunity to prosper from this transition, and to be a part of a future where the benefits of robotic labor are democratized as a legacy that can be shared by all, not being confined to benefit only the tech companies and the venture capital that fuels them.

FAQ

What are Language-Behavior Models?

These are the AI models that give humanoid robots their intelligence and understanding of the world around them. The models enable robots understand and respond to human actions and language while pursuing the goals they are tasked with. Also known as Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, this development in applied AI is opening the door to a future where most tasks can be performed by robotic servitors. The missing ingredient to making these models universal, adaptable, and intuitive is the large amount of training data enabling similar advances - such as the Large Language Models (LLMs) which have taken center stage in tech and are revolutionizing knowledge work.

How do you collect training data?

Trace mining partners gather data from real-world task interactions during their regular daily activities.  This data is then verified, labeled, and processed to create high value training data for robotic AI servitors.
